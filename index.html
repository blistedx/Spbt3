🏆 TOURNAMENT RULES & REGULATIONS

🏸 S.P. Badminton Tourney 3 · Official Pair Registration & Participation Agreement

1. Age & Category Eligibility

Both players of a pair must strictly belong to the exact same age category (Both Below 35 OR Both 35 & Above) determined by their official Date of Birth (DOB). Original Government-issued Photo ID (Aadhaar / Driving License / Passport) is mandatory for desk verification before your first match.

2. Single Team Entry Policy

A player is permitted to register in only one pair / team. Duplicate or substitute entries with different partners are strictly disallowed without prior written permission from the tournament director.

3. Entry Fee & Slot Confirmation

The official tournament entry fee is ₹1000 per team. Team slot remains in Pending Verification status until the payment UPI UTR / Reference number is verified. Once your registration is approved, the entry fee is strictly Non-Refundable.

4. Match Format & Scoring Rules

All matches follow direct single-elimination knockout format. Matches are Best of 3 Sets to 21 Points under standard BWF rally point rules (deuce with 2-point difference, max point cap at 30). Official tournament standard Yonex Mavis 350 shuttlecocks will be used.

5. Reporting Time & Walkover Policy

Both players must report to the tournament desk at Suryodaya Park at least 30 minutes before their scheduled fixture time. If a team fails to report on court within 15 minutes of their official match call, an automatic walkover (disqualification) will be awarded to the opponent.

6. Code of Conduct & Authority

Players must wear proper sports attire and non-marking badminton shoes. Unsportsmanlike conduct, verbal abuse, or property damage will result in immediate disqualification. The decisions of the On-Court Referee, Umpires, and the Organizing Committee are final, conclusive, and binding.
