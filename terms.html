Terms & Conditions

Official Match Rules, Eligibility Criteria, Code of Conduct & Tournament Regulations for S.P. Badminton Tourney Season 3.

Rule 01

🏸 1. Age Categories & Player Eligibility

The tournament is an open Men's Doubles competition organized into two distinct age divisions:

  • Below 35 Category: Both players in the pair must be strictly under 35 years of age based on their official Date of Birth (DOB).
  • 35 & Above Category: Both players in the pair must be 35 years of age or older.
Mandatory Desk Verification: Both players must carry their original Government-issued Photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Voter ID, or Passport) to the tournament desk before their first match. Any age discrepancy will lead to immediate disqualification without refund.
Rule 02

💳 2. Team Entry Policy & Fee Confirmation

  • Single Entry Restriction: A player is strictly allowed to participate in only one team/pair throughout the tournament. Dual entries or playing for multiple teams is prohibited.
  • Entry Fee: The official entry fee is ₹1000 per pair.
  • Slot Confirmation: Registration entries remain Pending Verification until payment transaction ID (UTR) is reconciled by the admin team.
  • Strict No-Refund Policy: Once verified and placed into the tournament draw, the registration fee is 100% non-refundable and non-transferable under any circumstances.
Rule 03

⚡ 3. Match Format & Scoring System

All matches follow the single-elimination straight-knockout tournament structure:

  • Set Format: Best of 3 Sets to 21 Points per set.
  • Rally Point Scoring: Standard BWF (Badminton World Federation) 21-point rally scoring system applies. At 20-all, the side which gains a 2-point lead first wins the set. At 29-all, the side scoring the 30th point wins the game.
  • Official Shuttles: Official tournament standard Yonex Mavis 350 (Green Cap) nylon shuttlecocks will be provided for all fixtures.
  • Intervals: A 60-second interval is allowed when the leading score reaches 11 points. A 120-second interval is allowed between sets.
Rule 04

⏰ 4. Reporting Time & Walkover Rules

Punctuality is crucial to ensure the schedule runs on time:

  • Both players of a pair must report to the official Suryodaya Park desk at least 30 minutes before their scheduled match fixture.
  • Walkover Grace Period: If a pair fails to report on court within 15 minutes after the second official court call, an automatic walkover (concession) will be awarded to the opposing team.
  • No rescheduling will be granted for late arrivals or personal schedule conflicts.
Rule 05

🤝 5. Sportsmanship & Code of Conduct

  • Players must wear proper athletic sportswear and non-marking badminton shoes on the court.
  • Aggressive arguments with line judges, umpires, organizers, or opponents, abusive language, or racket throwing will result in an immediate yellow card (warning), red card (fault), or black card (disqualification).
  • The decision of the On-Court Umpire and Chief Tournament Referee on any call or score is final, conclusive, and binding.
Rule 06

🩹 6. Health, Injury & Liability Disclaimer

Badminton is an active, high-intensity sport. By registering and stepping onto the court, players confirm they are medically fit to compete.

The S.P. Badminton Tourney organizing committee, sponsors, and venue administration assume no financial or legal liability for any bodily injury, muscle strain, dehydration, or medical emergency sustained during play, nor for loss or damage to personal belongings at the court. Basic first-aid kits and ice packs will be available at the tournament desk.
