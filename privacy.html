🛡️ Data Protection & Player Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: January 1, 2026 · S.P. Badminton Tourney Season 3 Organising Committee (Suryodaya Park).

Section 01

🏸 1. Introduction & Scope

At S.P. Badminton Tourney Season 3, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data of players, team managers, officials, and spectators. This Privacy Policy details how we collect, process, store, and safeguard your information when you register for the tournament, participate in matches, or use our digital portal (web app and courtside TV scoring system).

Key Commitment: We do NOT sell, rent, or commercialize your personal information to third-party marketers. Data is solely utilized for tournament administration, referee verification, match fixtures, and courtside broadcasting.
Section 02

📋 2. Information We Collect

When you register a men's doubles pair or interact with our system, we collect:

  • Player Identity Details: Full legal name, Date of Birth (DOB) for age verification, and t-shirt sizes.
  • Contact Information: Primary and partner mobile/WhatsApp numbers, and valid email addresses for fixture alerts.
  • Age Verification Proof: Government-issued photo ID (Aadhaar Card, Driving License, Voter ID, or Passport) inspected at the courtside registration desk to confirm entry in Below 35 or 35 & Above categories.
  • Payment & Financial Reference: UPI Transaction ID (UTR reference), payment screenshot, and timestamp to reconcile the ₹1000 entry fee. We do NOT store bank account numbers or PINs.
  • Match Telemetry & Statistics: Points won, games played, set history, and walkover records recorded by official court scorers.
Section 03

🎯 3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used strictly for legitimate tournament operations:

  • Assigning validated pairs to single-elimination tournament draws and bracket trees.
  • Generating digital Player Match Passes and QR codes for fast desk check-in.
  • Sending automated registration receipts and fixture call notifications via Email, WhatsApp, or SMS.
  • Broadcasting player names, team pairings, and real-time match points on the courtside TV screen and public website.
  • Awarding medals, certificates, and cash prizes to champions and runner-ups.
Section 04

📸 4. Media, Photography & Broadcast Rights

By participating in the S.P. Badminton Tourney 3 at Suryodaya Park, players and spectators acknowledge that high-definition photography, live score streaming, and video recordings take place on court.

The organizing committee reserves the right to capture match footage, highlight reels, and celebration photography for publication on the official tournament portal, social media channels, and community archives.

Section 05

🍪 5. Cookies & Local Storage

Our Progressive Web App (PWA) uses minimal, privacy-centric cookies and HTML5 Local Storage for essential application functionality:

  • Theme Preferences: Remembering your Dark or Light mode choice across sessions.
  • Offline Cache (Service Worker): Storing tournament draws, schedule, rules, and icons so you can access fixtures with poor network coverage at the court.
  • Scoring Sessions: Authenticating referee tablet scoring sessions during active matches.

You can review or adjust your preferences at any time by clicking Cookie Settings in the footer.

Section 06

🔒 6. Data Security & Contact Information

All communications between your browser and our server are secured with TLS encryption. Database access is restricted to authenticated tournament administrators.
